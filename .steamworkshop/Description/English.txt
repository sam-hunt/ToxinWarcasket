Toxin Warcasket

A new heavy chemical-warfare warcasket for [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2723801948]Vanilla Factions Expanded - Pirates[/url].

[h1]About[/h1]

The Toxin is a 7th generation warcasket that rebuilds VFE Pirates' Cataphract chassis for chemical warfare: the same superior plating and worse weight, and three pieces sharing one reagent tank. Flood a position with tox gas, breathe it unharmed, and draw it back into the tank when the work is done. Requires Biotech.

[hr][/hr]
[h2]The Set[/h2]
Cataphract-tier plating in all three pieces at a 7th generation price, unlocked by specialised warcaskets plus tox gas research. The full set has 100% toxic environment resistance: tox gas, fallout and pollution do nothing to it.
[list]
[*][b]Torso:[/b] the reagent tank, 100 chemfuel when full, refilled like a tox pack. Toggle the vent to empty it around the pilot as a growing cloud. 15% toxic environment resistance.
[*][b]Shoulder pads:[/b] a gas jet that sprays a cone of tox gas from the tank, with one reserve shot of its own. 15% toxic environment resistance.
[*][b]Helmet:[/b] the respirator. 80% toxic environment resistance and immunity to the tox gas debuff, like a gas mask, plus an ability that clears the air around the pilot and banks it in the tank.
[*][b]Downed or dead:[/b] a downed pilot bursts whatever is left in the tank; a dead one also pollutes the ground around the body.
[/list]

[h1]FAQ[/h1]
[b]Q: Do I need Biotech?[/b]
A: Yes. Tox gas and pollution only exist with Biotech, and every part of the set either makes or filters them.

[b]Q: How is it different from VFE Pirates' Hazard set?[/b]
A: The Hazard resists toxins that hit it. The Toxin breathes poisoned air: tox gas, fallout and pollution do nothing to the full set, but venom and toxic needles still do, and it brings its own cloud.

[b]Q: Does it work on mechanoids?[/b]
A: No. Mechanoids are immune to tox gas.

[b]Q: How do I get it?[/b]
A: Research specialised warcaskets and tox gas, then weld it on at the warcasket foundry like any other set. Mix and match the pieces with VFE Pirates' own sets as you please.

[b]Q: Was this made with LLMs?[/b]
A: Some of the grunt code, and most localizations. I'm a software engineer by trade, and have been writing code for 25 years. All code is manually reviewed and tested; all bug reports and mod-conflicts followed up personally as time and detail allows. All art is either commissioned from humans, hand-authored in Krita, or both.
