Toxin Warcasket

A new chemical-warfare warcasket for [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2723801948]Vanilla Factions Expanded - Pirates[/url].

[h1]About[/h1]

An impressively unethical feat of engineering, the Toxin is a 7th generation warcasket built on VFE Pirates' Cataphract chassis: Aerial-grade plating on a slightly lighter frame, and three pieces sharing a tox gas tank. Flood a position with gas, storm it unharmed, and recover a portion by clearing the air after. Requires Biotech.

[hr][/hr]
[h1]The Set[/h1]
[list]
[*][b]Torso:[/b] the tank, nearly three tox packs of gas, refilled with chemfuel the same way. Toggle the vent to empty it around the pilot as a growing cloud, and again to keep the rest.
[*][b]Shoulders:[/b] the gas jets. Each jet sprays a cone from the tank, four to a full tank, or two charges without one. The targeter shows where the gas lands and how far it drifts.
[*][b]Helmet:[/b] the respirator. Immunity to tox gas exposure, like a gas mask, plus the ability to suck gases around the pilot into the tank (clears smoke, rot stink, and deadlife dust too!).
[/list]

Mod settings allow tuning each ability and some stats. An opt-in toggle rewrites the whole set to the Spacer Warcasket tier instead: plating, shields, vacuum resistance, higher costs, and the later research gate.

[h1]FAQ[/h1]
[b]Q: Do I need Biotech?[/b]
A: Yes. Tox gas only exists with Biotech, and the whole set integrates with it.

[b]Q: How is it different from VFE Pirates' Hazard set?[/b]
A: The Hazard is the all-rounder: lighter, fireproof, and immune to toxic buildup and psychic effects, with thinner sharp/blunt plating. The Toxin is built for tox gas alone, in both offense and defense. It pays in weight for its stronger plating and tank, while still being more vulnerable to venom, fire, extreme temperatures etc. Opt-in settings give either set the spacer warcaskets' vacuum resistance, to set the two apart as you like.

[b]Q: How do I get it?[/b]
A: Research VFE Pirates' Specialised Warcaskets and Biotech's Tox Gas projects, then weld it on at the warcasket foundry like any other set. Mix and match the pieces with VFE Pirates' own sets as you please.
